Information architecture

Seven menu items, one level deep

Each role sees only its own menu. No sub-menus: second-level choices are tabs on the page. Breadcrumb plus the highlighted menu item always answer “where am I”; the trip progress bar answers “what's next”.

Every screen answers four questions

Where am I?
Highlighted menu item, breadcrumb, page title = the lane or record name.
What am I doing?
One task panel at the top of the detail page, titled as the task: “Choose a carrier”.
What can I do next?
One primary button labelled with the outcome: “Award to Kaveri · ₹42,500”.
What happens after?
A toast says what happened and what comes next: “Truck details will follow on WhatsApp”.
